This page compares results with the approved board budget dated 22 Feb 2025. Zone budgets are used for comparison only, and costs cover operating-site data in the database, not head-office overheads, depreciation, or finance costs. Revenue uses a fixed tariff of MK 1,450/m³. Statutory Compliance Status
Formal regulatory compliance should only be asserted where statutory laboratory indicators are available and validated.
Operational Proxy Context
Operational indicators remain useful for monitoring system conditions, but they do not replace regulated compliance evidence.
    Definitions & Method
    This module separates statutory compliance evidence from operational proxy context to preserve regulatory credibility and avoid misleading benchmark presentation.
